# 018 — more-animations — Requirements

## What's being built

A full catalogue of ready-made animations, in the three families motion tools use:
attention (loops), entrances and exits (once), plus text and group animations.

## Who it serves

Creators who want variety without building every motion with keyframes.

## Requirements

- R1@1: Attention animations, loopable: bounce, flash, pulse, rubber band, shake (x/y), swing, tada, wobble, jello, heartbeat, head shake.
- R2@1: Entrance animations: fade, slide, zoom, rotate, bounce, flip, back and light-speed, each from up, down, left or right where it applies.
- R3@1: Exit animations mirroring every entrance.
- R4@1: Text animations: typewriter, and reveal by letter, word or line with a stagger.
- R5@1: Group stagger: an entrance or exit applied to a group plays on its children one after another with a set gap.
- R6@1: Emphasis animations: highlight glow, underline draw and a spotlight focus.
- R7@1: Easings elastic, bounce and overshoot join the existing ones.
- R8@1: Each animation shows a live preview in the picker, and attention animations default to loop while entrances and exits default to once.
